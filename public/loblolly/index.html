A practical retreat for longer stays.

The Loblolly Suite is a private furnished one-bedroom home base for military transitions, traveling professionals, project assignments, relocations and extended visits. The suite includes an equipped kitchen, Wi-Fi and dedicated parking.

Its name reflects the loblolly pine — familiar, resilient and deeply tied to the landscape of this part of North Carolina.
