Your Fayetteville home base

Comfort for the stay you actually have.

Whether you're in Fayetteville for a military transition, medical assignment, project work, relocation or an extended visit, the Magnolia Suite is designed to make settling in straightforward. Suite 4 has a sizable kitchen, one full bathroom with a tub, and a bedroom furnished with a small loveseat for an in-room sitting area. There is no separate living room. Wi-Fi and dedicated parking round out the practical setup.

Magnolia takes its identity from one of the South's most recognizable blooms: understated, welcoming and timeless.
