Haymount · Fayetteville, NC

A furnished stay in a neighborhood you can actually live in.

Hinsdale House offers four private furnished one-bedroom suites at 122 Hinsdale Avenue in Haymount, close to downtown Fayetteville and surrounded by the restaurants, coffee, culture, churches and everyday services that make a longer assignment easier.

Why stay in Haymount?

For a week, location may simply mean commute time. For a month or more, the neighborhood matters. Haymount gives extended-stay guests a residential home base while keeping downtown Fayetteville and a useful collection of local businesses close at hand.

Our local guide intentionally concentrates on Haymount and downtown because a temporary home works better when dinner, coffee, a haircut, dry cleaning, a movie, a park or a night out do not require crossing the city.

Built for 30-day and extended stays

Each Hinsdale House suite includes a separate bedroom, furnished living space, equipped kitchen, in-unit washer and dryer, Wi-Fi and one dedicated off-street parking space.

That combination is especially useful for travel nurses and clinicians, military and defense personnel, consultants, project professionals and people relocating to Fayetteville.

For healthcare professionals working long shifts

When your schedule means several consecutive 12-hour shifts, the most valuable amenities are often the unglamorous ones: a kitchen waiting when you get home, laundry inside the suite, reliable Wi-Fi, dedicated parking and a neighborhood where food and everyday services are close by.

Hinsdale House is designed to make the hours outside work simpler rather than adding errands and hotel logistics to an already long day.

Haymount + downtown after work

Nearby options include local restaurants and coffee, the Cameo Art House Theatre, live theater, museums, Segra Stadium and the Fayetteville Woodpeckers, churches, salons, barbers, dry cleaning, running and walking resources and parks.

A residential alternative to an extended-stay hotel

Hotels can be useful for short trips. For longer stays, many guests value having a bedroom separated from their living space, the ability to prepare normal meals, private laundry and a residential setting.

Four one-bedroom suites

Choose among Magnolia, Dogwood, Loblolly and Hatteras. Availability changes by date, so send your full expected stay and preferred suite through the direct inquiry form.

Yes. We welcome inquiries for monthly and longer furnished stays, subject to availability.
