Monthly furnished rentals without the feel of a hotel.

Hinsdale House offers private one-bedroom furnished suites for guests who need a comfortable place for a month or longer in Fayetteville.

Designed for living, not just sleeping

A month is long enough to notice the difference between a hotel room and a real place to live. Hinsdale House gives extended-stay guests a private bedroom, furnished living or sitting area, equipped kitchen, laundry access, Wi-Fi and dedicated parking.

That setup works especially well for temporary assignments, relocations, home-sale gaps, military TDY or PCS transitions, healthcare contracts and project-based work.

Why monthly furnished housing can make sense

Move in without moving furniture

The suites are furnished, so you can arrive with personal belongings rather than outfitting an apartment for a temporary stay.

Cook normally

Each suite includes an equipped kitchen, making a 30-, 60- or 90-day stay more practical than relying on takeout.

Keep your routine

In-unit laundry, Wi-Fi and a dedicated parking space make it easier to maintain everyday routines while you are away from home.

Live in a real neighborhood

Hinsdale House is in Haymount near downtown Fayetteville, with local restaurants, coffee, churches, theaters, museums and everyday services concentrated nearby.

Who chooses monthly furnished rentals?

Typical extended-stay guests include travel nurses and clinicians, military personnel, government and defense contractors, consultants, construction and project professionals, relocating families, people between homes and visiting professionals who need more than a short hotel stay.

How long can I stay?

Availability varies by suite and date range. We welcome inquiries for approximately 30 days as well as longer stays. Submit your complete expected dates so we can evaluate the full stay at once.

Monthly rental vs. extended-stay hotel

The right choice depends on your needs. Hotels may make sense for very short stays or guests who want daily hotel services. A furnished apartment becomes more attractive when privacy, a real kitchen, laundry, separate living space and a residential setting matter more.

Four furnished suites

Hinsdale House has four individually named one-bedroom suites: Magnolia, Dogwood, Loblolly and Hatteras. Each has its own identity while sharing the same practical extended-stay foundation.

Frequently asked questions

Are utilities included?

Ask about the exact terms for your requested stay when you inquire. We keep the inquiry process direct so current pricing and included services can be confirmed for your dates.

Is there parking?

Yes. Each suite has one dedicated off-street parking space.

Is laundry in the unit?

Suites 1 and 2 include in-unit laundry. Suites 3 and 4 have shared laundry in the building.

Can I extend my stay?

Possibly, depending on future bookings. The earlier you know you may need an extension, the better.

Can I book directly?

Yes. Use the Hinsdale House availability form to send your dates and preferred suite directly.
