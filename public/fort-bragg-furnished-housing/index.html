Military stays · Fayetteville

Furnished housing for Fort Bragg assignments, transitions and the reset in between.

A private one-bedroom home base for PCS, TDY and other military-related stays when a hotel room is not enough.

Live normally while you're here.

Temporary military travel often lasts long enough that everyday basics matter. Hinsdale House offers furnished one-bedroom suites with equipped kitchens, Wi-Fi, laundry access and one dedicated off-street parking space per suite.

After 21 days in the field, the priorities change.

Sometimes the value of a temporary home becomes clearest when you return exhausted and dirty after an extended field period. You need to unload personal gear, shower, wash uniforms and clothing, dry boots and garments, eat real food, charge devices, sleep and reorganize before the next requirement.

A private suite with your own washer and dryer, kitchen, parking space and separate bedroom gives you a practical place to do that without treating a hotel room as a gear staging area.

Local resources for the refit

Our Recovery & Refit Resource Guide collects independent Fayetteville businesses that may help when normal self-service is not enough: military sewing and uniform setup, alterations and patches, professional garment cleaning, boot repair and shine, and local military/outdoor gear suppliers.

For personal-equipment organization inside the suite, we are evaluating practical additions such as boot trays, drying racks, sturdy hangers, drop-zone storage and boot dryers. Specialized equipment, hazardous materials and weapons should always be handled in appropriate authorized locations.

Useful for PCS and TDY stays

Guests can inquire for shorter or extended stays, select a preferred suite and provide assignment timing through our direct availability form.

A Fayetteville home base

Hinsdale House is at 122 Hinsdale Avenue in Fayetteville, giving military guests a residential base while they work, transition or wait for permanent housing.
