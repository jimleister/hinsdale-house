Culture, history & a night out

Things to do in Fayetteville

Airborne & Special Operations Museum

At 100 Bragg Boulevard downtown, the U.S. Army museum traces Airborne and Special Operations history from 1940 forward through artifacts and immersive exhibits. General admission is free. It is an especially worthwhile stop for military families, veterans and anyone interested in Fayetteville's Army history.

Official museum site →

Cameo Art House Theatre

This independent cinema at 225 Hay Street has served downtown for more than 25 years and screens a mix of independent, foreign, classic and mainstream films. It is a good alternative to a multiplex and an easy anchor for a downtown movie-and-dinner night.

Cameo showtimes & tickets →

Gilbert Theater

A downtown community theater at 116 Green Street presenting live productions in an intimate setting.

Gilbert Theater & tickets →

Cape Fear Regional Theatre

CFRT produces professional live theater and education programs in Fayetteville. Buy production tickets through the theater's official site.

CFRT shows & tickets →

Fayetteville Woodpeckers

Catch Fayetteville's Minor League Baseball team at Segra Stadium in downtown Fayetteville. The official team site offers single-game, season, group and specialty tickets.

Woodpeckers tickets →

Segra Stadium

The downtown ballpark at 460 Hay Street is home to the Woodpeckers and hosts additional events throughout the year.
