Arts, baseball & entertainment

Things to do in Fayetteville

Gilbert Theater

A downtown community theater at 116 Green Street presenting contemporary and classical productions. The theater sells tickets online and is located in the Fascinate-U building, with its entrance on the Bow Street side.

Gilbert Theater & tickets →

Cape Fear Regional Theatre

CFRT produces a five-show mainstage season along with education and outreach programs. Its current performance location is 1707 Owen Drive. CFRT advises patrons to purchase production tickets directly through its official website rather than third-party resellers.

Cape Fear Regional Theatre & tickets →

Fayetteville Woodpeckers

Catch Fayetteville's Minor League Baseball team at Segra Stadium, 460 Hay Street in downtown Fayetteville. The official team ticket page includes single-game, season, group and specialty ticket options.

Woodpeckers tickets →

Segra Stadium

The downtown ballpark is home to the Fayetteville Woodpeckers and also hosts special events. Check the team's official schedule and ticket pages when planning a game night.

Segra Stadium information →

Airborne & Special Operations Museum

For military history, the U.S. Army Airborne & Special Operations Museum is one of downtown Fayetteville's signature attractions and a natural stop for military families and visitors.

Museum information →

Eat local

Restaurants around Haymount and downtown Fayetteville

These are useful starting points for guests who want to get beyond national chains. Restaurant hours and menus change, so use the linked restaurant sites for current information.

Latitude 35

In Haymount at 1217 Hay Street, Latitude 35 is a laid-back local restaurant and bar with Caribbean and American influences, outdoor seating and weekend brunch.

Latitude 35 →

Haymount Truck Stop

At 100 Broadfoot Avenue, this local food-truck court and bar combines rotating food trucks with patios, drinks and an arcade. It describes itself as family- and pet-friendly.

Haymount Truck Stop →

Gaston Brewing Company

A veteran-owned downtown brewpub and restaurant at 124 Hay Street serving food alongside its craft beer program.

Gaston Brewing Company →

Circa 1800

At 108 Person Street downtown, Circa 1800 focuses on locally influenced Southern food and serves lunch, dinner and weekend brunch on its current schedule.

Circa 1800 →

Blue Moon Cafe

A downtown bistro at 310 Hay Street known for casual lunches, dinners, drinks and outdoor seating.

Blue Moon Cafe →

Pierro's Italian Bistro

A downtown Italian restaurant at 217 Hay Street with pizza, classic Italian dishes and a full-service dining setting.

Pierro's Italian Bistro →
