Your neighborhood · Haymount + downtown

Stay here. Live here. Most of what you need is right around you.

One of the advantages of Hinsdale House is its setting in Haymount, close to downtown Fayetteville. Rather than sending guests across the city, this guide deliberately concentrates on the neighborhood around the house and the nearby downtown core: coffee, dinner, theater, baseball, museums, parks, churches, haircuts and the practical stops that matter during a longer stay.

A compact home base

Think neighborhood, not hotel corridor.

Our goal is simple: show how much of daily Fayetteville life can happen in the Haymount–downtown area. The recommendations below are intentionally concentrated in this local core rather than scattered across Cumberland County. Many are along Hay Street, Arsenal Avenue, Oakridge Avenue, Broadfoot Avenue and the downtown blocks beyond.

Haymount

Historic homes, neighborhood restaurants, churches, salons, local shops and services give Haymount the feel of a real neighborhood rather than a commercial strip.

Downtown

Continue down Hay Street and you reach independent cinema, restaurants, museums, live theater, Segra Stadium, coffee and local businesses.

Leave the car parked sometimes

The property itself has a Walk Score of 60, classified as somewhat walkable, and the listing describes it as close to downtown and within walking distance of the Haymount shopping area and Festival Park.

Go farther only when you need to

Fort Bragg, Cape Fear Valley and destinations elsewhere in Fayetteville are easy reasons to leave the neighborhood. For everyday downtime, we want this guide to keep you local.

The point of the neighborhood

A longer stay shouldn't mean living out of your car.

Within the Haymount and downtown core you can get coffee, eat locally, see a movie or play, attend a baseball game, visit museums, worship, get a haircut, drop off dry cleaning, buy running gear and spend time outdoors. That concentration of everyday life is one of the reasons Hinsdale House works well for guests who are in Fayetteville for weeks rather than days.
