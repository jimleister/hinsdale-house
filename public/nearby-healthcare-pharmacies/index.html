Healthcare close to home · Fayetteville

Hospitals, urgent care and pharmacy resources nearby.

For an extended stay, knowing where to go when you need healthcare or a prescription matters. This guide separates hospital care, urgent care, outpatient services and pharmacy resources so guests can quickly find the right kind of help.

Hospital + outpatient care

Cape Fear Valley Highsmith-Rainey Specialty Hospital

Highsmith-Rainey Specialty Hospital is at 150 Robeson Street in downtown Fayetteville. Cape Fear Valley Health identifies it as the region's long-term acute care hospital and a comprehensive outpatient-services provider. The facility is open 24/7.

Long-term acute care

Highsmith-Rainey provides specialized hospital care for patients with complex needs who require longer hospital stays.

Urgent care

Haymount Urgent Care

At 420 Owen Drive, Haymount Urgent Care provides both urgent care and primary care. Its current website lists walk-in care Monday-Friday, 9 a.m.-5 p.m., without an appointment. Services described include treatment for illnesses, lacerations and broken bones plus diagnostic testing.

Concentra Fayetteville

At 1702 Owen Drive, Concentra operates a weekday walk-in medical center and physical-therapy location. This can also be useful for occupational-health needs. Confirm current hours before leaving.

Pharmacy resources

Stewart Compounding Pharmacy

A locally operated pharmacy at 101 Broadfoot Avenue in Haymount. Stewart specializes in compounded medications and states that it accepts insurance coverage. Current published hours are Monday-Friday, 9 a.m.-5 p.m.; confirm directly before visiting.
