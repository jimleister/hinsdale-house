Arts + higher education · Fayetteville

A furnished home base for the work that brings you to Fayetteville.

Private one-bedroom furnished suites for visiting faculty, guest lecturers, university professionals, performers, directors, designers, musicians, technicians and other arts or academic professionals here for weeks rather than years.

Performing artists & production professionals

Cape Fear Regional Theatre operates a five-show mainstage season and recruits performers as well as production overhire in scenery, costumes, lighting and sound. For out-of-town artists and production professionals spending a rehearsal or performance period in Fayetteville, a furnished one-bedroom suite can provide a residential alternative to an extended hotel stay.

Why a suite works for production life

Rehearsal and performance schedules can make a hotel room wear thin quickly. A separate bedroom, living space, equipped kitchen, private laundry, Wi-Fi and dedicated parking give a performer or production professional a place to maintain a normal routine between calls.

Visiting faculty & university guests

Fayetteville's higher-education community includes Fayetteville State University, Methodist University and Fayetteville Technical Community College. Temporary furnished housing may fit visiting faculty, adjunct instructors, guest lecturers, consultants, interview candidates, trainers and newly hired employees relocating to the area.

Hinsdale House is not positioned as conventional student housing. The stronger fit is an adult professional or university guest who needs a furnished residence for a defined assignment or transition.

Haymount and downtown between workdays

Hinsdale House is in Haymount near downtown Fayetteville, giving guests access to restaurants, coffee, theater, museums, parks and everyday services when they are off the clock.
