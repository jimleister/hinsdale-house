Travel nurse housing in Fayetteville that feels like a home, not a hotel.

Private furnished one-bedroom suites for travel nurses, clinicians, medical contractors and other healthcare professionals who need a comfortable place for a few weeks, a month or an extended assignment.

Furnished housing built around assignment life

Hinsdale House offers four individually named furnished suites at 122 Hinsdale Avenue in Fayetteville. Each suite gives one guest or a couple a private place to settle in, prepare meals and maintain a normal routine while working away from home.

Instead of relying on a hotel room for an entire assignment, guests have a separate bedroom, living space, an equipped kitchen, Wi-Fi, in-unit laundry and a dedicated off-street parking space.

Housing for Cape Fear Valley assignments

Cape Fear Valley Medical Center is the flagship hospital of Cape Fear Valley Health and is located at 1638 Owen Drive in Fayetteville. Hinsdale House is positioned for medical professionals working at the hospital or elsewhere in the Fayetteville healthcare community.

Healthcare travelers may also be assigned to clinics and facilities throughout the system, so we recommend confirming your specific work location when evaluating any furnished rental.

Built for the hard weeks, too

Some assignments are not three neat shifts followed by four easy days. If you have just finished five 12-hour shifts, home should not hand you another list of errands. Private laundry lets scrubs go straight into the washer. An equipped kitchen gives you control over food. Dedicated parking removes one more task at the end of a long day.

We also maintain a Recovery & Refit Resource Guide with independent local resources for healthy prepared meals and in-home pet care - two services that can matter disproportionately during consecutive long shifts.

What medical travelers usually need

A real kitchen

Long assignments are easier when every meal does not require takeout. Each Hinsdale House suite includes an equipped kitchen for everyday cooking and meal preparation.

Private laundry

In-unit laundry makes uniforms, scrubs and everyday clothing easier to manage around changing schedules and long shifts.

Reliable Wi-Fi and living space

Wi-Fi supports streaming, personal business and staying connected, while a separate living area gives you somewhere to decompress outside the bedroom.

Dedicated parking

Each suite has one dedicated off-street parking space, removing the uncertainty of searching for parking when returning from work.

Flexible furnished stays in Fayetteville

Medical assignments do not always fit a standard twelve-month lease. Hinsdale House welcomes direct inquiries for shorter and extended furnished stays. Tell us your arrival date, expected departure date and preferred suite, and we can respond based on current availability.

If your contract dates change, contact us as early as possible so we can discuss whether an extension or adjustment is available.

Who this housing may fit

Our furnished suites can work well for travel nurses, allied health professionals, physicians, medical contractors, visiting clinicians and other professionals temporarily working in the Fayetteville area. The same setup also works for corporate travelers, military personnel and people relocating to Fayetteville.

Frequently asked questions

Are the suites furnished?

Yes. Hinsdale House is designed as furnished housing, so guests do not need to move furniture into the property for an assignment-length stay.

Do the suites have kitchens?

Yes. Each suite includes an equipped kitchen intended for normal day-to-day meal preparation.

Is laundry available?

Yes. The suites include in-unit laundry.

Is parking included?

Each suite has one dedicated off-street parking space.

Can I stay for more than one month?

Yes, depending on availability. Submit your complete assignment dates so we can evaluate the full stay rather than only the first month.

How do I check availability?

Use our direct availability form and include your dates, number of guests and preferred suite. You can also compare the Magnolia, Dogwood, Loblolly and Hatteras suites before submitting your inquiry.
