Recovery + refit · Fayetteville

When work takes everything out of you, home should make the reset easier.

This guide is built around two demanding realities: finishing a run of five 12-hour healthcare shifts, or returning after weeks in the field. These Fayetteville resources can help reduce errands, restore routines and get you ready for what comes next.
