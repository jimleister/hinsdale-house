Privacy

Website privacy notice

Hinsdale House collects only the information needed to understand website use and respond to stay inquiries.

Stay inquiries

When you submit the availability form, you provide information such as your name, contact details, dates, party size, suite preference, reason for stay and any message you choose to include. This information is used to respond to your inquiry and evaluate availability.

Form processing

The inquiry form is processed through FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service. Information entered into the form is transmitted through that service so Hinsdale House can receive and respond to the inquiry.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate website activity such as page views, traffic sources and interactions. Hinsdale House does not intentionally send the name, email address, phone number or free-text inquiry message you submit to Google Analytics.

Third-party links

The website may link to third-party services such as Furnished Finder. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Your choices

You can browse the site without submitting the inquiry form. Browser privacy settings and content blockers may also limit analytics collection.

Questions

For a privacy question, use the stay inquiry form and select “Other” as the reason for your message.
