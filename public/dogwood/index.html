A comfortable place to land

Private space for everyday living.

The Dogwood Suite gives military guests, traveling professionals, relocating families and extended-stay visitors a private furnished one-bedroom home base in Fayetteville. An equipped kitchen, Wi-Fi and dedicated parking make longer stays easier.

Named for North Carolina's state flower, Dogwood is intended to feel bright, familiar and welcoming from the day you arrive.
