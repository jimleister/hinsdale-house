A comfortable place to land

Private space for everyday living.

The Dogwood Suite gives military guests, traveling professionals, relocating families and extended-stay visitors a private furnished one-bedroom home base in Fayetteville. Suite 3 has a sizable kitchen, one full bathroom with a tub, and a bedroom furnished with a small loveseat for an in-room sitting area. There is no separate living room. Wi-Fi and dedicated parking help make longer stays easier.

Named for North Carolina's state flower, Dogwood is intended to feel bright, familiar and welcoming from the day you arrive.
