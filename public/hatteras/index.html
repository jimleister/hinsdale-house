The Hatteras Suite offers a private furnished one-bedroom space for military guests, medical travelers, project professionals, relocations and extended stays in Fayetteville. An equipped kitchen, Wi-Fi and dedicated parking help make day-to-day life simple.

Hatteras draws its character from North Carolina's Outer Banks: relaxed, straightforward and connected to one of the state's most distinctive places.
