# Hinsdale House Referral & Direct-Booking Targets

Updated: 2026-09-20

## Purpose
Build repeat referral relationships with organizations that regularly bring temporary professionals to Fayetteville. The pitch is simple: four private furnished one-bedroom suites in Haymount for defined assignments, with kitchens, in-unit laundry, Wi-Fi and dedicated parking.

## Priority outreach

### Performing arts
**Cape Fear Regional Theatre (CFRT)**
- Audience: out-of-town performers, directors, designers, stage management, musicians, technicians and production overhire.
- Why: CFRT operates a mainstage season and recruits performers and production personnel; temporary housing is a recurring production need.
- Outreach path: producing/artistic administration, company management, production management.
- Offer: one contact for current suite availability and direct assignment-length inquiries.
- Website: https://www.cfrt.org/

### Higher education
**Fayetteville State University**
- Audience: visiting faculty, candidates, guest lecturers, consultants, researchers and relocating hires.
- Outreach path: Human Resources, Academic Affairs/provost offices, department administrators, procurement/travel where appropriate.
- Website: https://www.uncfsu.edu/

**Methodist University**
- Audience: visiting academics, candidates, professional-program guests, consultants and relocating hires.
- Outreach path: Human Resources, Academic Affairs, department/program administrators.
- Website: https://www.methodist.edu/

**Fayetteville Technical Community College**
- Audience: short-term instructors, trainers, consultants, candidates and visiting education professionals.
- Outreach path: Human Resources, academic/program leadership, workforce training.
- Website: https://www.faytechcc.edu/

### Healthcare
**Cape Fear Valley Health**
- Audience: travel nurses, allied health professionals, physicians, contractors, locums/temporary clinicians and relocating hires.
- Relevant facilities include Cape Fear Valley Medical Center and Highsmith-Rainey Specialty Hospital.
- Outreach path: recruitment/talent acquisition, staffing, department administrators and travel-clinician coordinators where applicable.
- Website: https://www.capefearvalley.com/
- Hinsdale resource: https://hinsdalehousenc.com/travel-nurse-housing-fayetteville-nc/

### Military, defense & government support
**Fort Bragg ecosystem**
- Audience: TDY personnel where appropriate, civilian trainers, technical representatives, defense contractors, consultants and project personnel.
- Outreach path: contractor HR/travel coordinators, program/project managers, recruiting and relocation teams.
- Do not imply DoD/Fort Bragg endorsement or partnership.
- Hinsdale resource: https://hinsdalehousenc.com/fort-bragg-furnished-housing/

### Project / engineering / construction / utilities
- Audience: inspectors, engineers, construction managers, superintendents, commissioning personnel, environmental staff, utility/telecom teams and consultants.
- Outreach path: project managers, field-services leaders, HR/recruiting, travel coordinators and project controls.
- Hinsdale resource: https://hinsdalehousenc.com/corporate-housing-fayetteville-nc/

### Secondary / episodic sources
- Insurance catastrophe-response and restoration teams.
- Attorneys, expert witnesses and temporary government/legal personnel.
- Film/media/production crews.
- Coaches, trainers, officials and temporary athletic staff.
- Families supporting longer medical stays.
- Relocating professionals waiting for permanent housing.

## Outreach message
Keep outreach relationship-first rather than discount-first:
1. Introduce Hinsdale House and the four-suite format.
2. Explain the assignment types it is designed around.
3. Link directly to the relevant landing page.
4. Offer a simple way for the coordinator to ask about dates and current availability.
5. Ask to be kept as a local furnished-housing resource for future temporary assignments.
6. Never describe an organization as a partner until an actual relationship exists.

## Tracking
For every organization record: organization, contact name, role, email/phone, audience type, date contacted, response, next follow-up, referred guest, booked nights and revenue. Use distinct UTM links by organization when sending website links so GA4 can identify referral traffic.
