# Hinsdale House Professional Photography Brief

## Objective
Create one professional image library that works for hinsdalehousenc.com, Furnished Finder, Airbnb and future direct-booking marketing.

## Property-wide shots
- Exterior hero image in the best available natural light
- Straight-on exterior establishing shot
- Angled exterior showing character and scale
- Entrance and arrival experience
- Property signage / Hinsdale House branding once installed
- Parking layout showing convenient dedicated parking
- Attractive exterior architectural details
- Any outdoor seating or guest-use areas

## Every suite: Magnolia, Dogwood, Loblolly, Hatteras
Capture the same core sequence for consistency.

### Living area
- Wide hero from the strongest corner
- Reverse wide showing room flow
- Medium composition emphasizing seating and comfort
- One or two tasteful detail shots

### Kitchen / dining
- Full kitchen wide
- Opposite angle showing layout
- Dining/work surface
- Appliances and useful amenities in context

### Bedroom
- Hero from doorway/corner
- Reverse angle
- Bed-centered composition
- Storage/closet if visually useful
- Bedside/detail image

### Bathroom
- Clean wide showing layout
- Vanity/mirror composition
- Shower/tub composition
- Detail shot only if it adds value

### Practical features
- Workspace if present
- Laundry access if applicable
- Entry/door experience
- Dedicated parking relationship where useful

## Deliverables requested
- 12–18 strong finished images per suite
- 6–10 property-wide/exterior images
- Primarily horizontal/landscape images
- At least 3–5 vertical images per suite for mobile/social use
- High-resolution originals plus web-ready exports
- Consistent white balance and natural-looking editing
- No heavy HDR, extreme wide-angle distortion or artificial sky replacement

## Website hero requirements
The strongest exterior/property image should have enough negative space on the left or lower-left for the Hinsdale House headline and buttons. Also capture an alternate hero with negative space on the right.

## Styling before photographer arrives
- Remove cords, remotes, trash cans and cleaning products from primary compositions
- Make beds tightly and consistently
- Open blinds/curtains consistently
- Turn on practical lamps as directed by photographer
- Clear kitchen counters except intentional styling
- Put toilet lids down and remove bathroom personal/cleaning items
- Use restrained North Carolina-themed accents; avoid clutter
- Check bulbs for matching color temperature
- Clean windows, mirrors, stainless steel and reflective surfaces

## Priority
If time is limited, prioritize: exterior hero, living room, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, then details. Every suite needs at least one excellent image of each primary room before spending time on decorative close-ups.
